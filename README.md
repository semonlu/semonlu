## About me / 关于我

I'm **Renjie Lu (路仁杰)**, a clinician and **PhD candidate in Orthopedics and Traumatology of Traditional Chinese Medicine** at **Shanghai University of Traditional Chinese Medicine**, based in the **Department of Rehabilitation Medicine, Longhua Hospital**.

My research sits at the intersection of **musculoskeletal rehabilitation, clinical research, medical AI evaluation, and biomedical data science**. During a research internship at **Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (2025–2026)**, I contributed to medical AI evaluation and the **MedBench v4** research effort.

> I aim to connect real clinical needs with rigorous evaluation, interpretable methods and reproducible computational research.

**中文简介：** 上海中医药大学中医骨伤科学博士研究生，上海中医药大学附属龙华医院康复医学科临床与科研工作者。研究聚焦肌骨康复、医疗人工智能评测、临床研究及生物医学数据科学。
